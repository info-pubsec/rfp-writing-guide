# RFP Writing Guide — Winning Canadian Government Proposals

The evaluation grid is the entire game. Most losing proposals are written against what the bidder wanted to say, not what the grid scores.

## The process that wins

1. **Extract the evaluation grid** — what criteria, what weights, what's mandatory vs rated
2. **Build a compliance matrix** — every requirement mapped to your response section
3. **Draft against the grid** — your proposal structure mirrors the evaluator's checklist
4. **Write the executive summary last** — when you know what you actually committed to

## Key statistics that should change your approach

- **Construction RFPs average 3.1 bidders; services 1.8** — competition varies 2× by category
- **75% of service tenders receive one bid** — the incumbent usually wins; displacement requires a genuine opening
- **29.3% of federal awards are non-competitive** — becoming known to the buyer before the formal process matters more than proposal polish

## Common disqualification triggers

- Missing a mandatory requirement (buried on page 47)
- Unverifiable claims without named references
- Wrong format (page count, font, structure requirements)
- Late submission (even by minutes)
